**Project name**

Personalised Mentor

**Tagline**

Know what to study next. See why. Watch the plan adapt.

**Problem and solution**

A student has two hours and a pile of chapters. Personalised Mentor turns that into an explained next step: rank the topics, show the evidence, quiz from cited material, then change the plan after the score. Different exams are the product goal; the verified demo is one fictional board-exam student, not proof of universal exam support.

**Concrete workflow and evidence**

The fictional-only local browser and terminal conversation ask a fictional student to clarify her exam, ranks synthetic topic records and explains the recommendation with data-row references. Rule-built quizzes cite local NCERT chapter notes. A 4/5 quiz moves Electrostatics mastery from 0.35 to 0.485; repeating completion saves no second score. A later 5/5 result moves mastery to 0.64 and changes the next topic to Integrals. The student can inspect and forget local H-MEM summaries. The Oct 28 builder-recorded typed browser run persisted both attempts, and forgetting memory summaries preserved the scores. Browser-build typecheck, 43 focused tests and 1 HTTP test passed. Earlier Oct 27 offline journey/chat/control runs and 13/13 H-MEM conformance passed. The historical broader scoped run had 302 passing, 2 failing and 2 skipped tests, with two externally dependent files excluded. All demo students, scores, weights and dates are fictional; research is mocked and planning is deterministic.
