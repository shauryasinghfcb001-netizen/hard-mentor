=== Turn 5: "What is Coulomb's law?" ===
Reply: The force between two point charges q1 and q2 a distance r apart is proportional to the product of the charges and inversely proportional to r squared: F = k |q1 q2| / r^2, acting along the line joining them. In SI units k = 1/(4 pi epsilon_0), about 9 x 10^9 N m^2 C^-2, and the permittivity of free space epsilon_0 is about 8.854 x 10^-12 C^2 N^-1 m^-2. Like charges repel and unlike charges attract.

Source: NCERT Physics Part I, Class 12, Ch 1, §1.5, pp. 6-8
